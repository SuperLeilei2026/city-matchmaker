# MiniMax H3 生成提示词

MiniMax 当前网页生成器单段设置为16:9、2K、15秒，因此把视觉隐喻拆成多个可剪辑镜头。生成画面不承担产品事实展示；真实 UI 在后期加入。每段都不要求模型绘制中文或品牌 Logo。

## 统一角色与风格锚点

在每段提示词前复用：

> Original cute near-future railway station world, cinematic product-film motion design, 16:9. Two original guide mascots: a small charcoal-black cat conductor with a cyan ticket punch, and a small cream-white curly dog conductor with a magenta signal flag. Rounded handmade silhouettes, expressive but subtle, no resemblance to existing copyrighted characters. Deep navy industrial station, cyan and magenta twin rail lines, tiny signal-yellow accents, clean negative space, premium soft 3D mixed with tactile paper textures, playful and intelligent rather than childish. No logos, no legible text, no subtitles, no watermarks, no fake app UI.

## 01 开场车票

> A blank ivory graduation train ticket floats in a pure white studio. It lands with a soft paper bounce and cleanly unfolds into two glowing rail lines, cyan to the left and magenta to the right. The black cat conductor and white curly dog conductor peek from behind the ticket, exchange one curious look, then each follows a different line. Smooth macro camera move, restrained charming character animation, crisp product-ad lighting. End on both tracks entering a dark navy station portal with open center space for later title overlay.

## 02 每个选择都像对的

> Inside the miniature station, four elegant destination lightboxes rise around one undecided young graduate silhouette holding a suitcase. Shiny numerical score tokens rain down and stack into a confusing tower; the cat taps the tower and the numbers harmlessly break into paper confetti. The dog rolls out two blank destination tickets instead. Camera circles from crowded signs to a calm symmetrical twin-platform composition. No readable text or numbers in the final frame; leave center space for typography.

## 03 把现实装上车

> A playful assembly line at the station: four physical blocks symbolizing career, a walking path and book, a rent wallet, and a humidity warning droplet click into a transparent train carriage one by one. Each block visibly changes the route map lights; the yellow humidity block becomes a strict signal gate rather than a decorative preference. The cat checks the career block, the dog checks the daily-life block. Satisfying magnetic motion, precise object animation, no words or user interface.

## 04 三幕双城 PK

> Split-platform duel without combat: cyan train on the left, magenta train on the right. Three overhead gates illuminate in sequence, representing career, daily life, and landing cost through simple icons. At every gate the central lever stays exactly in the middle, showing uncertainty as a valid result. The two mascots do not choose sides; they hold clipboards and reveal transparent evidence cards, counterexample cards, and blank unknown cards. Energetic tracking shot, rhythmic signal lights, no text.

## 05 决胜提问官

> A tiny friendly signal-room agent appears as an original round brass-and-glass question-lantern, not humanoid and not a robot brand mascot. It scans the two routes but does not move either train. It finds one blinking yellow humidity signal and projects exactly one blank question card plus one blank task tag. The graduate must press a physical confirmation stamp before the tag enters the luggage list. Clear cause and effect, warm character reactions, premium product film.

## 06 双城通行证

> Close-up of an original dual-city passport booklet opening on a station desk. One cyan route receives a “first to verify” visual stamp made only of abstract symbols, while the magenta route stays visibly active as backup. Three luggage tags appear for job, housing, and everyday-life checks. The train begins moving on the cyan track while the magenta platform lights remain on. Cat and dog wave small flags; final wide shot leaves generous dark navy negative space for title and call to action. No legible text.

## 生成负面约束

> Avoid cyberpunk city clichés, neon overload, weapons, combat, dystopia, humanoid robots, anime IP resemblance, extra limbs, deformed paws, unreadable typography, random letters, visible brand logos, fake dashboards, exaggerated lens flares, frantic cuts, and camera shake.
